Spy Chess is chess with one twist. Before the first move, each player secretly recruits three of the opponent's pieces.

Rule 1 of 4

Recruit

Before the game starts, choose three of your opponent's pieces in secret. They are your spies.

White's spies: Ne7, Bb6, h7

Rule 2 of 4

Activate

When the moment is right, reveal a spy. It changes sides on the square where it stands.

Move 9. The knight on e7 turns

Rule 3 of 4

Command

A revealed spy moves and captures for you like any of your own pieces.

Rule 4 of 4

Limit

Three spies per player. Kings can never be recruited.

Questions

Do I need to know chess first?

Spy Chess uses the standard chess moves, so it helps to know them. The only new rules are the four on this page.

Can a king be a spy?

No. Kings can never be recruited.

How many spies does each player get?

Three. Each player recruits three of the opponent's pieces before the first move.

What happens when a spy pawn promotes?

It is still your spy, queen and all.

Where can I play?

In your browser at spychess.games, on iOS as Spy Chess: Genesis, and on Android through Google Play.
